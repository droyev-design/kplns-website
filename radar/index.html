מה אומרות הרמות

0 · שקטלא נדלק אף מדד. זו אמירה על החיישנים, לא על המציאות.
1 · מוגברמשפחת מדדים אחת נדלקה. מדד בודד הוא לרוב רעש — לא סיבה לשנות תוכניות.
2 · אזהרהשתי משפחות התנהגות שונות נדלקו יחד, משני ספקי נתונים נפרדים לפחות, ולפחות אחת מהן אות פעולה. שווה לשים לב לחדשות.
3 · קריטישלוש משפחות ומעלה, משלושה ספקים נפרדים לפחות — או שינוי במדיניות ההתגוננות של פיקוד העורף, שמספיק לבדו. עקבו אחרי ההנחיות הרשמיות.

הרמה סופרת כמה משפחות מדדים נדלקו יחד — לא כמה חדשות פורסמו. המשפחות מודדות התנהגויות שונות, אבל חלקן מגיעות מאותו ספק נתונים, ולכן הספירה עוברת מסנן גיוון-ספקים: רמה 2 ומעלה מחייבת שני ספקים נפרדים לפחות, ורמה 3 מחייבת שלושה ספקים נפרדים לפחות. בנוסף, כל מדד מסווג פעולה — צעד שעולה למבצע שלו משהו וחושף ידיעה (שינוי הנחיות התגוננות, ניתוק אינטרנט, מרחב אווירי שמתרוקן, שיבוש GPS) — או דעה — כסף שמהמר על סמך אותן חדשות (שווקים, שוק ניבוי, רמות אזהרת מסע). רמה 2 ומעלה מחייבת לפחות אות פעולה אחד: דעות מאשרות ומחזקות, אבל לבדן הן לעולם לא מייצרות אזהרה. מ-1.9.2026 שלוש משפחות הדעה (שווקים, שוק ניבוי, אזהרות מסע) עברו להקשר ואינן מצביעות כלל — הכלל נשמר בקוד כמנגנון בטיחות למקרה שמשפחת דעה תחזור להצביע, אבל היום אין לו על מה לחול. לכן הרמה נעה לאט, ובכוונה.

What the levels mean

0 · QuietNo tripwire detected. That is a statement about the sensors, not about the world.
1 · ElevatedOne indicator family lit. A single indicator is usually noise — not a reason to change plans.
2 · WarningTwo different behaviour families lit together, across at least two separate data providers, at least one of them an action signal. Worth following the news.
3 · CriticalThree or more families, from at least three separate providers — or a change in Home Front Command defence policy, which is sufficient on its own. Follow the official instructions.

The level counts how many indicator families lit together — not how many headlines were published. The families measure different behaviours, but some of them arrive over the same data provider, so the count passes through a provider-diversity guard: level 2 or higher requires at least two separate providers, and level 3 requires at least three separate providers. Each indicator is also typed action — a step that costs its actor something and reveals knowledge (a civil-defence policy change, an internet cut, an airspace emptying, GPS jamming) — or belief — money betting on the same news everyone reads (markets, the prediction market, advisory levels). Level 2 and up requires at least one action signal: beliefs confirm and add confidence, but on their own they never manufacture a warning. Since 2026-09-01 all three belief families (markets, the prediction market, travel advisories) moved to context and cast no vote at all — the rule is kept in the code as a safety property in case a belief family is ever given a vote again, but today it has nothing to bind. That is why it moves slowly, by design.

שיגור טילים או מטח אינם נראים באף אחד מהמקורות החינמיים שהדף הזה קורא. זו מגבלה של המקורות, לא של הכיול, והיא נובעת מהרכב הרשימה: מעל איראן אין כיסוי מקלטי ADS-B כלל, ומעל ישראל אותו ערוץ סופר מטוסים אזרחיים באוויר — לא גופים בטיסה בליסטית. זיהוי תרמי מלוויין (NASA FIRMS) נמדד על יום ה-UTC המלא האחרון בלבד, כלומר בפיגור של יממה, והוא רואה משהו שבוער אחרי פגיעה. קובצי שיבוש ה-GPS יומיים אף הם ומתפרסמים בפיגור של כיום. מחירים זזים רק אחרי שהידיעה כבר ציבורית, ושער הדולר-שקל כאן הוא השער היציג של בנק ישראל — פרסום אחד ליום עסקים. הערוץ המהיר ביותר ברשימה, קישוריות האינטרנט של איראן, נדגם בכל ריצה — אבל הוא מודד אם מדינה מחוברת, לא אם משהו שוגר.

לכן 0 כאן פירושו "החיישנים האלה שקטים", ולא "השמיים שקטים". המקור המחייב היחיד לשיגור הוא פיקוד העורף.

⊘ What a 0 means — and what it does not

Level 0 says one thing: the indicators listed on this page crossed its threshold. It is not a claim that nothing moved — an indicator can move a long way without crossing, and context families are not counted at all. It is not a claim that nothing is happening.

A missile launch or a salvo is not visible in any of the free data sources this page reads. That is a limit of the sources, not of the calibration, and it follows from what the roster is made of: over Iran there is no ADS-B receiver coverage at all, and over Israel that same channel counts civil aircraft in the air — not bodies on a ballistic trajectory. Satellite thermal detection (NASA FIRMS) is measured over the newest complete UTC day only, so it runs about a day behind and it sees something burning after an impact. The GPS-interference files are daily too, and are published about a day behind. Prices move only once the news is already public, and the USD/ILS rate here is the Bank of Israel's representative rate — one publication per business day. The fastest channel on the roster, Iranian internet connectivity, is sampled every run — but it measures whether a country is online, not whether something was launched.

So a 0 here means "these sensors are quiet", not "the sky is quiet". The only authoritative source for a launch is the Home Front Command.

🇮🇷 The Iran index has a ceiling of 2, not 3

Since 2026-09-01 the Iran index rests on two voting families over two data providers — Iran's national internet connectivity (IODA) and regional military aviation (adsb.lol). Level 3 requires at least three separate providers, so it is not reachable here. The Iran index's ceiling is 2, and the page says "of 2".

The other rows on the Iran panel — satellite thermal anomalies, Hormuz GPS interference, the markets and the prediction market — are still measured, still stored, and still shown with their live thresholds, but they cast no vote. The reason is what can be measured over Iran on free channels: thermal detection is damage assessment after a strike, not a warning before one; Gulf GPS jamming is a maritime signal, outside the question this page asks; and markets and betting odds are opinion, formed from the same news every other collector reads. None of the three meets the bar for a leading indicator, so none of them gets a vote.

The two families that do vote are not in better shape in practice: merit-nt's bar today is below 24, and the lowest value ever retained across the 14 stored state files is 26.4; the military-aviation bar is more than 14 aircraft, and the highest value ever measured in those same files is exactly 14. This is a statement about what we can measure, not about Iran. We would rather publish a declared ceiling than a dial that looks like 0-3 and cannot move.

How this works. The system does not sum up news headlines. It tracks costly actions — ones that cost somebody something to take: a change to civil-defence instructions, an airspace emptying of civil aircraft, a state cutting its own internet, a currency collapsing, a travel advisory being raised. Statements and headlines light nothing here. "Costly" itself splits in two: an action costs the actor something and so reveals what it knows; a belief costs a bettor money and proves only that the bet is serious. The level rises as more indicator families light together — families that measure different behaviours and, from level 2 up, are read from at least two separate data providers and include at least one action; beliefs alone stop at level 1. A single indicator is usually noise. Indicators tagged regional count for both indices.

Methodology

Every indicator is boolean — lit or unlit — not a score. The level is simply how many indicator families are lit, after a provider-diversity guard. There is no weighted average and no subjective scoring, because an average lets news noise push the dial when nothing has actually happened.

How the level is decided
  • 0 · Quiet — no family lit.
  • 1 · Elevated — one family. (Until 2026-09-01 this also said: any number of belief families lighting with no action signal stops at 1. Since 2026-09-01 there are no voting belief families — markets, the prediction market and travel advisories moved to context, so they contribute no vote at all. The rule itself stays in the code.)
  • 2 · Warning — two families, from at least two separate data providers, at least one of them an action family.
  • 3 · Critical — three or more, from at least three separate providers, or a change in Home Front Command defence policy, which is sufficient alone: it is the most expensive and most Israel-specific action in the set.

The provider-diversity guard. The families measure different behaviours, but they are not independent observations: three arrive over adsb.lol, two over a single State-Department fetch (since 2026-09-01 only embassy_alerts of those two votes), and the two GPS-interference families over one gpsjam daily file (since 2026-09-01 only il_gps of those two votes, so that pair is no longer a correlation risk — it is still written here because both rows are still displayed from the same file). One glitch at one provider could otherwise manufacture a 🔴 that looks like three separate corroborations. So level 2 or higher requires at least two distinct providers, and level 3 requires at least three distinct providers. The guard can only lower a level, never raise one. (A third cap — "at most two votes between families sharing a provider" — was deleted from the code on 2026-09-01 after all 131,072 possible states were enumerated and it never changed an outcome.) One documented exception: a Home Front Command defence-policy change is sufficient alone — it is not a claim of corroboration across three sources, but a single authoritative act of the Israeli government.

The action/belief guard. Every family is typed in code as action (costly to whoever performs it, and so revealing what they know) or belief (costly to a bettor who reads the same news as everyone else). Level 2 and up requires at least one lit action signal; beliefs confirm and add confidence, but on their own they stop at level 1. This guard, too, can only lower a level, never raise one — and when any guard has capped the level, the page says which, under the gauge it bound. The type is still shown on every row, and the guard has been dormant since 2026-09-01: all three belief families moved to context, so every voting family is an action family and there is nothing left to cap.

Indicators tagged "regional" count for both indices. An indicator that could not be read cannot light — so when a source fails, an explicit warning is shown, because otherwise the page would look calmer than reality.

The safeguards that prevent false alarms
  • Baseline before alerting — a median-based rule is inert until enough samples have accumulated (about six hours from start).
  • Presence is not change — the system alerts on deviation from a baseline, not on the mere existence of a value. One deliberate exception: Home Front Command defence policy stays lit for as long as a restrictive instruction is in force. A restrictive instruction is a state that persists, not just the moment it was announced — an indicator that goes dark after half an hour while the restrictions are still in force is not a preparation layer.
  • The baseline is frozen during an event — a lit indicator stops feeding its own history, so a sustained event cannot erase the rule that detects it. The hold is capped at 72 hours and is shown on the indicator's row.
  • Provider diversity — level 2 or higher requires at least two separate data providers, and level 3 requires at least three. (Until 2026-09-01 this also said families on one provider contribute "at most two votes between them". That cap was deleted from the code on 2026-09-01 after all 131,072 possible states were enumerated and it never once changed an outcome — the two requirements that remain already block the same case.)
  • Beliefs never manufacture a warning alone — "belief" families confirm and reinforce, but without at least one lit "action" signal the level stops at 1. Since 2026-09-01 all three belief families moved to context and cast no vote at all (markets, the prediction market, travel advisories), so this rule is dormant: there is no belief family left that could be counted. It stays in the code because a new belief family, or restoring a vote to one of the three, makes it live again immediately.
  • Two-observation confirmation — travel advisories are measured against a feed that is unstable between requests, so a change must repeat twice in a row before it counts.
  • What cannot be measured is declared, not shown as a green dial.
  • A second state accumulator: "is the build-up continuing?" (2026-09-01) — every other rule here is a change detector: a value against a rolling median of its own recent past. That shape has one structural blind spot, and the stored data contains a textbook instance of it: Hormuz GPS interference sat at 10–14% from 9 to 28 Aug 2026 — four to five times its own level a week earlier — and the page showed 🟢 on every one of those twenty days, because by day three the rolling median had moved up with it. Since 2026-09-01 every action family is also scored against a declared anchor: a fixed number, written in the code with its provenance and date, that does not move when the world moves. The row then carries "N of the last 14 days above the declared anchor". It casts no vote and raises no level — a sustained state is not an event. And the caveat, said plainly: every anchor was measured inside the war (the oldest sample on disk is from 2026-08-01; the war has been open since 2026-02-28), so they can say "more than the August-2026 norm" and not "more than peacetime".
  • Two standing figures beside the level (2026-09-02) — the level answers "what changed", and only that. Since 2026-09-02 both forms are printed at the top of each index, side by side. Standing state: how many of that index's anchored families are past their declared anchor today, and how many days out of 14 the leading family has been — in the exact sentence its own row prints, from the same code, so a headline cannot contradict a row. It reports the count of days and not the streak: on 2026-09-01 the streak was one day while the row itself said 12 of 14, and both were correct — the day before last was under the bar. Standing daily risk: the daily probability implied by the open Polymarket contract — a market's probability, not a forecast by this page — normalised to a per-day rate rather than shown as the contract price, because a contract price falls on its own as expiry approaches. Both are displayed and neither votes. When too few days are stored, or the prediction market was not read, that is written where the number would have been — a missing figure is not an all-clear.
Known limits
  • There is no ADS-B receiver coverage over Iran, so there is no Iranian counterpart to the airspace indicator.
  • Israeli NOTAMs are read since 2026-09-01, from the Israel Airports Authority's Aeronautical Information Service page — and they are shown for context only, with no vote, until they have a measured baseline. There is no NOTAM channel for Iran or the wider region: every free channel tested is blocked from this server (FAA, ICAO, autorouter and more). That half of the gap is declared, not filled with a guess.
  • There is no public Jordanian NOTAM/AIS source. Checked 2026-09-01: www.carc.jo, ais.carc.jo and aim.carc.jo all fail to resolve in DNS (NXDOMAIN). This is stated separately because the page does count aircraft over Jordan, which invites the assumption that it also sees a Jordanian closure notice. It sees none of them. Israeli NOTAMs are read as normal since 2026-09-01 and are not part of this gap.
  • There is no ship-traffic (AIS) measurement in the Strait of Hormuz — UKMTO is blocked and the NGA feed has been frozen since 2024. The GPS interference indicator measures a different phenomenon (electronic warfare, aviation-derived) and is not a substitute for shipping data — the shipping channel is simply unmonitored, and that is declared as a gap.
  • Thermal anomalies do not identify a cause. A large heat signature can be a strike, a wildfire or agricultural burning, and the indicator cannot tell them apart. It counts "something large is burning where nothing usually burns", and no more. A measurement on 16 Aug 2026 found roughly 800–1,300 anomalies a day in the region, the overwhelming majority of them permanent gas flares over the Khuzestan oil fields — which is why the indicator filters out locations that burn continuously.
  • ADS-B coverage over Jordan exists but is thin — 5–15 aircraft at a normal moment, vs ~80 over Israel (measured 17 Aug 2026). The Jordan indicator therefore demands nearly-empty skies — a sum of at most 3 aircraft across 3 checks. A partial closure cannot be detected here: a sustained drop to 2 aircraft, roughly 60% of the traffic, is indistinguishable from noise at these counts under every calibration tested. That is a limit of the signal itself.
  • An internet drop in a neighbouring state is not necessarily a war signal: maintenance, a power failure and censorship of domestic protest look identical from outside. The indicator reports that something big happened to a state's connectivity — not why.
  • The GPS-interference indicator over Israel arrives about a day behind (a daily file), and cannot tell who is jamming or why: Israeli defensive jamming, hostile interference and civil equipment faults look identical in it. It is a days-level preparation-layer signal, not a minutes-level one — and intermittent jamming is the standing background over Israel, which is why the threshold sits above the whole envelope measured in a calibration month.
  • The prediction market is real money, but still an opinion, not an action: the bettors mostly read the same news the other collectors read, so it is a confirmation channel, not early warning. The figure shown is a risk per day, normalized to the contract's horizon — not a headline "percent chance of a strike".
  • In a fast scenario, none of the indicators may move before the event.
  • The system checks every 30 minutes. It is not continuous.
