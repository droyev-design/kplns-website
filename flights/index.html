⚠️ What this page does not know

  • This is not a cancellation feed and not an official source. Passengers must check with their airline.
  • The official board sees only about 3 days ahead. A suspension announced for next week will not show here until it enters that window.
  • An airline that quietly removes flights from the schedule (without marking them cancelled) shows up only as a falling carrier count — not as cancellations.
  • A handful of cancellations is routine operations — 0–2% measured on an ordinary day. A small number of cancellations means nothing.
  • This is a private project, not an official body. The output is a level — not a forecast and not a probability.

1 · חריגה במדד אחדמדד בודד נדלק. לרוב רעש תפעולי — לא סיבה לשנות תוכניות.

הרמה סופרת כמה מדדים בלתי תלויים נדלקו יחד — לא כמה כותרות פורסמו. חברת תעופה זרה שמפסיקה לטוס לישראל מפסידה כסף אמיתי; לכן זה איתות שקשה לזייף, לשני הכיוונים — גם החזרה של חברות היא איתות רגיעה אמיתי.

What the levels mean

0 · Normal operationsNo indicator lit. Board and traffic within their usual range.
1 · One indicator litA single indicator fired. Usually operational noise — not a reason to change plans.
2 · Disruption formingTwo independent indicators lit together. Worth following.
3 · Major disruptionAll three indicators lit — a broad aviation stoppage. Check with your airline before travelling to the airport.

The level counts how many independent indicators lit together — not how many headlines were published. A foreign carrier that stops flying to Israel loses real money; that makes it a hard-to-fake signal in both directions — carriers returning is an equally genuine calm signal.

למה דווקא זה. חברת תעופה זרה שמשעה טיסות לישראל מקבלת החלטה יקרה: הכנסות אבודות, מקומות חניה (slots) אבודים, צוותים שצריך להחזיר. היסטורית ההחלטה הזו מקדימה הסלמה ולא עוקבת אחריה — והחזרה של החברות היא איתות רגיעה אמיתי באותה מידה. לעומת זאת, אל על ממשיכה לטוס גם כשכולם עוצרים — ולכן ספירת מטוסים גולמית מסתירה בדיוק את מה שחשוב. הדף עוקב אחרי מספר החברות הזרות השונות, לא אחרי מספר הטיסות.
Why this, specifically. A foreign airline suspending Tel Aviv service makes an expensive decision: lost revenue, lost slots, crews to reposition. Historically that decision leads escalation rather than following it — and carriers returning is an equally genuine calm signal. El Al, by contrast, keeps flying when everyone else stops — which is why a raw aircraft count hides exactly the thing that matters. This page tracks the number of distinct foreign carriers, not the number of flights.

The window stays fixed at 7 days even when the history is shorter, so the page never implies it has been measuring all week. This window holds 152 real samples, the first at 20/08/2026 19:07 and the last at 23/08/2026 22:37. The line is a step line: a level is drawn as held from the sample that measured it until the next one. No smoothing, no averaging, no invented points — every vertex is a row that exists in the data file. A gap of more than 90 minutes between samples cuts the line and is hatched: a hole in the data is not calm.

בעיית השבת והלילה — ואיך היא נפתרת

התעופה בישראל קורסת בכל שישי בערב מסיבות שאין להן שום קשר לביטחון: החברות הישראליות — כמחצית מהתנועות — אינן טסות בשבת, ובכל לילה יש עוצר טיסות. נמדד בנתוני הרדאר העצמי (60 מייל ימי): 25.6 שעות ניטור ב-17–18.8.2026, ימי חול בלבד, n=55 מדידות פרוסות על 24 תאי-שעה (2–6 מדידות לתא — היסטוריה קצרה בלבד, לא חודשים). טווח 4–32 מטוסים, חציון כולל 13. חציון שעות הלילה (23:00–05:00, n=14) הוא 8.5 מול חציון שעות היום (n=41) 15 — כלומר שפל הלילה הרגיל הוא כ-57% מחציון היום, קרוב מסוכן לסף התרעה נאיבי. לכן שום דבר כאן לא מושווה לקו בסיס כלל-שבועי:

  • מדידת האוויר מושווית רק לאותה שעה באותו סוג יום — קריאת ליל-שישי נשפטת רק מול לילות-שישי קודמים.
  • ספירת החברות הזרות מושווית רק לימים מאותו סוג (חול / שישי / שבת).
  • מדד הביטולים הוא אחוז מתוך הלוח של אותו יום עצמו — ולכן שבת מקוצרת אינה יכולה להזיז אותו מעצם היותה מקוצרת.
  • תא זמן ללא מספיק היסטוריה אינו נשפט — תאי שישי ושבת אינם יכולים להתריע לפני שנצפו לפחות 3 ימי שישי ו-3 שבתות אמיתיים.

הדף פותח ומתוחזק בעזרת כלי AI, בפיקוח אנושי. כמו בראדאר ההסלמה — מה שלא ניתן למדוד מוצהר כפער, ולא ממולא בניחוש.

Methodology

Every indicator is boolean — lit or unlit — and the level is simply how many are lit. No weighted average, no scoring. A rule that does not yet have a baseline is not allowed to fire — it is shown as "building baseline" until it has real history to compare against.

What each indicator measures, and what lights it
IndicatorSourceWhat lights it
Foreign airlines on today's boardOfficial Ben Gurion board (data.gov.il, IAA) The number of distinct foreign carriers with a non-cancelled flight today falls below 60% of the median of comparable days (Sun–Thu / Friday / Saturday judged separately). Needs 4 comparable days of history.
Flight cancellationsOfficial Ben Gurion board 10% or more of today's or tomorrow's flights cancelled, and at least 20 flights — and only if the condition repeats on two consecutive checks. Measured norm: 0–2% a day.
Aircraft airborne around Ben Gurionadsb.lol (ADS-B broadcasts) The count falls below 35% of the median for the same hour on the same day-class. A bucket without 6 prior samples, or with a median under 5, is not judged at all.
The Shabbat and night problem — and how it is solved

Israeli aviation collapses every Friday evening for reasons that have nothing to do with security: Israeli carriers — roughly half the movements — do not fly on Shabbat, and every night there is a curfew. Measured before this radar's own 60 nm data: 25.6 hours of monitoring on 17-18 Aug 2026, weekdays only, n=55 samples spread across 24 hour-buckets (2-6 samples per bucket — short history, not months). Range 4-32 aircraft, overall median 13. Night-hour (23:00-05:00, n=14) median is 8.5 vs day-hour (n=41) median 15 — the ordinary night trough alone is ~57% of the daytime median, dangerously close to a naive alert threshold. Therefore nothing here is ever compared against an all-week baseline:

  • The air measurement is compared only against the same hour on the same day-class — a Friday-night reading is judged only against previous Friday nights.
  • The foreign-carrier count is compared only against days of the same class (weekday / Friday / Saturday).
  • The cancellation indicator is a share of that same day's own board — so a shortened Shabbat schedule cannot move it by being shortened.
  • A time bucket without enough history is not judged — Friday and Saturday buckets cannot fire before at least 3 real Fridays and Saturdays have been observed.
Numbers measured before go-live (17 Aug 2026)
  • 49–54 distinct foreign airlines per day on the Ben Gurion board (65 operators in the window overall).
  • Roughly 750–830 flights a day (arrivals and departures).
  • Cancellations on an ordinary day: 0 to 15 flights (0%–1.8%) — mostly single operational cancellations of one carrier to one destination.
  • Aircraft airborne within 60 nautical miles (this radar's own measurement, 17-18 Aug 2026): range 4-32 aircraft over 25.6 hours of monitoring, weekdays only, n=55 samples (2-6 per hour-bucket — short history only). Overall median 13; night-hour median 8.5 vs day-hour median 15.
What was tested and rejected, and what this page does not measure
  • The IAA website's own API sits behind an anti-bot challenge — the data comes from the open, official mirror of the same board on data.gov.il, which updates within minutes.
  • FlightAware, aviationstack and aviation-edge require paid keys — tested (401 / "Missing API Key") and not used.
  • This page cannot see airline suspension announcements (press releases, customer emails) — only what actually appears on the board.
  • The air measurement includes aircraft overflying Israel en route elsewhere.
  • It is not continuous.

This page is developed and maintained with AI tooling, under human review. As on the escalation radar — what cannot be measured is declared as a gap, not filled with a guess.
